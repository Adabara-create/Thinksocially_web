01 — Risk Assessment

Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

Thinksocially performs a security assessment of your organization to determine if you are vulnerable to hacking, and recommends solutions for any problem areas. Malicious digital hackers are an increasing risk for today's businesses. Their actions can include defacing your website or Internet presence, stealing your intellectual property, or accessing your proprietary client information. If you work with large corporate customers or government entities, they may also target your clients by stealing their information from your systems. Our risk assessment goes beyond just updating and patching your system — we also address key vulnerabilities and predict problems before they can happen.

On request, Thinksocially will implement an evaluation to help you create a more secure environment for your organization. Our review includes, but is not limited to:

  • Computer Access — Audit logs, access control lists, password usage, and password effectiveness
  • Security Policy — Policy existence, adherence to industry standards, internal policy adherence, and security training
  • Security Status — Patch levels, network and workstation vulnerability, security configurations for hardware and software, anti-virus software, routers and firewalls, wireless passwords and settings, remote access security, and two-factor authentication
  • Data Integrity — Data storage security, data backup, and disaster recovery
